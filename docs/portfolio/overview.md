# Portfolio Management

## Institutional Quant Platform

---

# Purpose

The Portfolio Management module is responsible for transforming investment signals into an optimized, risk-aware portfolio.

It integrates alpha signals, factor scores, market regime information, investment constraints, and risk models to construct portfolios that maximize expected returns while maintaining acceptable levels of risk.

---

# Objectives

The Portfolio module is designed to:

- Construct optimal portfolios
- Maximize risk-adjusted returns
- Control portfolio risk
- Maintain diversification
- Enforce investment constraints
- Minimize turnover
- Reduce transaction costs
- Generate executable portfolios
