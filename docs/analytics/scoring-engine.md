# Scoring Engine

## Institutional Quant Platform

---

# Purpose

The Scoring Engine combines normalized factor scores, alpha signals, market regime adjustments, and configurable business rules into a single composite investment score.

This composite score is used to rank securities and provide consistent, explainable inputs to the Portfolio Optimizer.

---

# Objectives

The Scoring Engine is designed to:

- Combine multiple investment factors
- Generate a unified investment score
- Produce transparent security rankings
- Support configurable scoring methodologies
- Adapt scores to changing market regimes
- Provide explainable investment decisions

# Factor Weighting

Each factor contributes to the composite score.

Example

| Factor | Weight |
|----------|-------:|
| Value | 20% |
| Growth | 20% |
| Momentum | 20% |
| Quality | 15% |
| Profitability | 10% |
| Liquidity | 5% |
| Financial Strength | 5% |
| Volatility | 5% |

Weights are configurable.

---

# Composite Score

Conceptually

```text
Composite Score

=

Σ (Factor Score × Factor Weight)

+

Alpha Adjustment

+

Regime Adjustment

+

Business Rule Adjustments
```

---

# Regime Adjustments

The scoring engine dynamically adjusts factor weights.

Example

## Bull Market

Increase

- Momentum
- Growth

Reduce

- Defensive Factors

---

## Bear Market

Increase

- Quality
- Low Volatility

Reduce

- Momentum

---

## Sideways Market

Increase

- Value
- Dividend Yield
- Quality
