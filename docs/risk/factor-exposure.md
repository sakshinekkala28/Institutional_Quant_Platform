# Factor Exposure Analysis

## Institutional Quant Platform

---

# Purpose

The Factor Exposure Engine measures how sensitive a portfolio is to systematic investment factors.

Rather than evaluating securities individually, the engine analyzes the portfolio at an aggregate level to determine whether performance is primarily driven by Value, Growth, Momentum, Quality, Size, Volatility, Liquidity, or other common risk factors.

Understanding factor exposure helps maintain diversified portfolios, control unintended bets, and improve risk-adjusted returns.

---

# Objectives

The Factor Exposure Engine is designed to

- Measure portfolio factor exposure
- Detect unintended factor bets
- Support diversification
- Improve portfolio construction
- Monitor factor drift
- Assist risk budgeting
- Support active risk attribution

# Exposure Calculation

Portfolio exposure is calculated as the weighted contribution of each security.

Conceptually

```text
Portfolio Exposure

=

Σ

(Security Weight × Factor Score)
```

---

# Active Factor Exposure

Active exposure compares the portfolio to its benchmark.

```text
Active Exposure

=

Portfolio Exposure

−

Benchmark Exposure
```

Positive values indicate an overweight factor position.

Negative values indicate an underweight factor position.

# Factor Drift

Factor exposure is continuously monitored.

Triggers include

- Portfolio Rebalancing
- Market Movements
- Regime Changes
- Corporate Actions

Significant drift may trigger portfolio review or rebalancing.

# Factor Diversification

The engine evaluates

- Number of active factors
- Dominant factor
- Factor concentration
- Factor correlation
- Exposure balance

This helps prevent excessive reliance on any single investment style.

---

# Regime Awareness

Factor preferences change according to market regimes.

| Regime | Preferred Factors |
|----------|------------------|
| Bull | Growth, Momentum |
| Bear | Quality, Low Volatility |
| Sideways | Value, Dividend |
| High Volatility | Defensive, Quality |
| Low Volatility | Growth, Momentum |

The engine reports both current exposure and recommended exposure based on the detected market regime.
