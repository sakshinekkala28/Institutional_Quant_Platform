# Order Management System (OMS)

## Institutional Quant Platform

---

# Purpose

The Order Management System (OMS) is responsible for managing the complete lifecycle of every order generated by the Institutional Quant Platform.

It acts as the central control layer between portfolio management and external broker or exchange execution systems, ensuring orders are validated, routed, monitored, modified, and audited throughout their lifecycle.

The OMS is designed to support institutional trading workflows with high reliability, traceability, and operational efficiency.

---

# Objectives

The Order Management System is designed to

- Generate executable orders
- Validate all orders
- Route orders to execution venues
- Track order status
- Manage partial fills
- Handle modifications and cancellations
- Maintain complete audit history
- Support multiple brokers

# Order Types

Supported order types

---

## Market Order

Execute immediately at the best available price.

---

## Limit Order

Execute only at or better than the specified price.

---

## Stop Order

Triggered after reaching the stop price.

---

## Stop-Limit Order

Triggers a limit order once the stop price is reached.

---

## Iceberg Order

Large order divided into multiple smaller visible orders.

---

## Fill or Kill (FOK)

Must be executed immediately in full or cancelled.

---

## Immediate or Cancel (IOC)

Execute immediately; cancel any unfilled quantity.

---

## Good Till Cancelled (GTC)

Remains active until executed or cancelled.

# Partial Fill Management

If an order is partially executed

- Remaining quantity is tracked
- Average execution price is updated
- Portfolio positions are adjusted incrementally
- Execution algorithms continue until completion or cancellation
